## FAQ

**Is this really free?**  
Yes. The audit is free, no signup, no credit card. Credex makes money when teams hire us to actually implement the changes — renegotiate contracts, migrate API providers, consolidate seats. If you never reach out, you never pay anything.

**What tools do you cover?**  
8 tools right now: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. We add new ones regularly — all pricing is verified weekly against official pricing pages.

**How does it actually find savings?**  
Two things happen per tool. First, it checks if there's a cheaper plan on the tool you're already using that covers your usage — like Cursor Pro vs Business based on your team size. Second, it looks for functional overlaps where you're paying for two tools that do the same job.

**Do you store my data?**  
No. Your inputs aren't saved anywhere. If you choose to download a PDF or generate a shareable link, we store a stripped-down version of the report with no email or company name. If you skip that, nothing is saved at all.

**Why not just use a spreadsheet?**  
You could. But the spreadsheet can't tell you that Cursor Pro covers your use case just as well as Business for a team of 2, or that you're paying for three coding assistants with overlapping functionality. It also won't write you a summary explaining why each change makes sense. And it'll take a weekend instead of 60 seconds.
